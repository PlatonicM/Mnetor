Privacy Policy

Last Updated: October 2026 • Effective Date: October 1, 2026

1. Introduction

At Mentor Platform ("we", "our", or "us"), we are committed to protecting your privacy and ensuring the security of your personal information. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard your data when you visit our website, enroll in course tracks, or interact with our learning management services.

2. Information We Collect

We collect information that you voluntarily provide to us when creating an account, enrolling in courses, or submitting lab assignments:

  • Personal Identity Data: Name, email address, profile avatar, and account authentication credentials.
  • Academic & Telemetry Data: Course enrollment history, lesson progress, quiz scores, certificate issuance records, and lab repository links.
  • Transaction Data: Order history, billing invoices, and payment status (note: full credit card credentials are processed securely via encrypted third-party payment gateways).

3. How We Use Your Information

We utilize collected data solely for legitimate educational and administrative purposes:

  • To provision and synchronize your course curriculum progress.
  • To issue verifiable digital certificates upon course completion.
  • To enable 1-on-1 mentor reviews of your project lab submissions.
  • To send essential account updates, real-time OTP verifications, and newsletter insights.

4. Data Protection & Security

We implement industrial-grade security measures including SSL/TLS encryption, secure password hashing, 2FA OTP verification, and strict database access controls to prevent unauthorized access, alteration, or disclosure of your personal data.

5. User Rights & Account Erasure

You maintain full ownership of your data. You may request account deletion at any time through your account settings or by requesting a deletion OTP. Permanently deleting your account removes all associated profile data, enrolled tracks, and certificates from active storage.
